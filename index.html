Data & Method

What in this console is real, derived, estimated, or unavailable — and where the underlying pipeline needs strengthening before operational use.

🛰️ Spill detection & geometry real

Sentinel-1 SAR scene (2018-09-26), segmented with a trained U-Net (ResNet34 encoder). Area, length, and origin coordinates are computed from the actual predicted mask — origin is the mask centroid, reprojected from the scene's native UTM CRS to WGS84.

⏱ Age & thickness classification heuristic

Age uses a constant-spreading-rate approximation, not a validated physical model. Thickness class comes from a mean/std SAR-intensity threshold. Both are indicative signals, not measured or lab-validated values.

🌊 Drift hindcast / forecast real inputs simplified model

Copernicus Marine ocean current + wind fields for the correct scene date (2018-09-26) at the derived origin. The advection itself is a single-point, constant-velocity extrapolation — not yet the particle-ensemble / time-varying-field model this pipeline is designed to grow into.

👁 EO (Sentinel-2) corroboration inconclusive

A real Sentinel-2 scene was found 1 day from the SAR acquisition (S2B_16RBT_20180925_0_L2A). 97% of the AOI was cloud/shadow-obscured — the pipeline correctly reported "inconclusive" rather than fabricating a spectral match.

🚢 AIS vessel data real

MarineCadastre AIS extract for 2018-09-26, filtered to the Gulf region and to exclude moored/anchored, low-speed, and small vessel types. 238 vessels retained after filtering.

🎯 Candidate scoring heuristic weights

Proximity, timing, AIS-gap, route-deviation, and rendezvous signals, combined with manually-set weights. Route deviation is currently a straight-line-ratio (not a historical-corridor baseline); rendezvous is a proximity-in-time-bucket check (not a full approach→dwell→separation sequence). Both are known first-pass implementations pending refinement.

✉ Notifications demo mode

Alerts created in this console are queued in local demo history, not delivered externally, unless a real SMTP/SMS provider is wired into the backend (see the Notification Composer for connection notes).
